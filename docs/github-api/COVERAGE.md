## Gestures

- Hand a pallet to the worker who asked for it ("je prends"), or take it yourself
  `graphql.mutation.addAssigneesToAssignable`
- Rope two pallets: this one waits for that one (Gérard's crane is busy)
  `graphql.mutation.addBlockedBy`
- Talk to a worker (wake the one in the hammock, answer a question): a comment, read back before it is sent
  `graphql.mutation.addComment`
- Tag a pallet (trade, level, bug) and catch the termites: bug triage
  `graphql.mutation.addLabelsToLabelable`
- Chalk a sketch on the site board: a draft item
  `graphql.mutation.addProjectV2DraftIssue`, `graphql.mutation.updateProjectV2DraftIssue`
- Bring a pallet onto the site: add an issue or PR to the board
  `graphql.mutation.addProjectV2ItemById`
- Cheer a worker: a reaction on an issue or comment
  `graphql.mutation.addReaction`, `graphql.mutation.removeReaction`
- Link two related pallets (a dotted chalk line)
  `graphql.mutation.addRelatesTo`, `graphql.mutation.removeRelatesTo`
- Deliver the mayor's parcel to the right building: attach the issue to its epic
  `graphql.mutation.addSubIssue`
- Clear finished pallets to the archive yard, or bring one back
  `graphql.mutation.archiveProjectV2Item`, `graphql.mutation.unarchiveProjectV2Item`
- Unload a pallet from the sprint truck, clear its size
  `graphql.mutation.clearProjectV2ItemFieldValue`
- Lay the last floor: close as completed (the magic moment); or send a duplicate or a not-planned pallet back
  `graphql.mutation.closeIssue`
- Turn a chalk sketch into a real pallet (an issue)
  `graphql.mutation.convertProjectV2DraftIssueItemToIssue`
- Order a new pallet: the lead writes a delivery note, it lands in the stock
  `graphql.mutation.createIssue`
- The evening bulletin: post the board's status update (on track, at risk) after the radio recap
  `graphql.mutation.createProjectV2StatusUpdate`, `graphql.mutation.updateProjectV2StatusUpdate`
- Pin a pallet on the site noticeboard (the repository's pinned issues)
  `graphql.mutation.pinIssue`, `graphql.mutation.unpinIssue`
- Free a pallet nobody is carrying any more (back to the stock)
  `graphql.mutation.removeAssigneesFromAssignable`
- Cut the rope: the pallet no longer waits
  `graphql.mutation.removeBlockedBy`
- Remove a wrong tag
  `graphql.mutation.removeLabelsFromLabelable`
- Move a parcel to another building
  `graphql.mutation.removeSubIssue`
- A crack in a floor: reopen a closed pallet
  `graphql.mutation.reopenIssue`
- Reorder the pallets of a building
  `graphql.mutation.reprioritizeSubIssue`
- Send the inspector: ask someone to review a pull request
  `graphql.mutation.requestReviews`, `graphql.mutation.requestReviewsByLogin`
- Bring back a pallet wrongly sent back as a duplicate
  `graphql.mutation.unmarkIssueAsDuplicate`
- Rewrite a delivery note, tick the inspector's checklist (DoR), place a pallet under a milestone crane
  `graphql.mutation.updateIssue`
- Fix what you said: edit your own comment
  `graphql.mutation.updateIssueComment`
- Say what a pallet is: task, feature, bug, epic
  `graphql.mutation.updateIssueIssueType`
- Push a pallet to another zone (status column), load it on the sprint truck (iteration), set its size
  `graphql.mutation.updateProjectV2ItemFieldValue`
- Reorder a zone: put the most urgent pallet in front
  `graphql.mutation.updateProjectV2ItemPosition`
- Keep an eye on a pallet: subscribe to its notifications
  `graphql.mutation.updateSubscription`
- Empty the site office mailbox: mark notifications read or done
  `rest.activity/mark-repo-notifications-as-read`, `rest.activity/mark-thread-as-done`, `rest.activity/mark-thread-as-read`
- Set up a mayor's visit: create or move a milestone (REST only; GraphQL has no milestone mutation)

## Reads

- Refresh one pallet or a handful after a gesture, by id
  `graphql.query.node`, `graphql.query.nodes`
- The board (Projects v2): items, status, iteration, size; the members and their avatars
  `graphql.query.organization`, `graphql.query.repositoryOwner`, `graphql.query.user`
- The API budget left, shown discreetly so the game never runs dry
  `graphql.query.rateLimit`
- The site: issues, sub-issues, labels, milestones, pull requests, assignees and their avatars
  `graphql.query.repository`
- Find pallets across the site (the search bar at the site office)
  `graphql.query.search`
- Who is playing: the lead's login and avatar
  `graphql.query.viewer`
- The lead's mail at the site office: notifications for this repository
  `rest.activity/get-thread`, `rest.activity/list-notifications-for-authenticated-user`, `rest.activity/list-repo-notifications-for-authenticated-user`
- What happened on the site since the last visit: feeds the morning radio
  `rest.activity/list-repo-events`
- Who can carry a pallet: only these workers appear when handing one over
  `rest.issues/check-user-can-be-assigned`, `rest.issues/check-user-can-be-assigned-to-issue`, `rest.issues/list-assignees`
- The story of a pallet (who moved it, when) and what changed since the last visit
  `rest.issues/get-event`, `rest.issues/list-events`, `rest.issues/list-events-for-repo`, `rest.issues/list-events-for-timeline`
- Render a delivery note or comment the way GitHub shows it, inside the pallet card
  `rest.markdown/render`
- The board as the team sees it in a saved view
  `rest.projects/list-view-items-for-org`, `rest.projects/list-view-items-for-user`
- What a pull request brings, on the inspection board (files and commits counted)
  `rest.pulls/check-if-merged`, `rest.pulls/list-commits`, `rest.pulls/list-files`
